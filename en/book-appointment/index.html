Book an appointment at the physiotherapy clinic in Temsia, Agadir

Physiotherapy Clinic — Temsia, Agadir Book an appointment

Pick a time below that works for you with Houda or Noureddine Gouizine. If you'd rather book by phone, call +212 6 59 73 02 83.

What happens on the first session?

Your first consultation at the Gouizine clinic in Temsia (Agadir) usually lasts 45 to 60 minutes. It begins with a thorough interview (symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, expectations) followed by a clinical exam: joint range, muscle strength, posture, painful movements. Houda or Noureddine then walks you through the physiotherapy assessment, the goals of care, and the expected number of sessions.

What to bring

  • Your medical prescription (if one was issued)
  • Your recent imaging (X-ray, MRI, ultrasound, CT) — on film or USB drive
  • Your surgical reports if you have had surgery
  • Comfortable clothing that lets us mobilize the area being treated (shorts and t-shirt for the lower body, tank-top for the upper, etc.)
  • Your CNSS / private insurance card if you'd like direct billing

Cancelling or rescheduling

To cancel or reschedule, please give us at least 24 hours notice by phone (+212 6 59 73 02 83) or email (info@kineagadir.com). That lets us free the slot for another patient on the waiting list.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a prescription to book?

A doctor's prescription is generally required for CNSS or private-insurance coverage. For wellness care, comfort sessions or clinical Pilates, no prescription is required.

How long is a session?

Follow-up sessions run 30 to 60 minutes depending on the type of care (physiotherapy, massage, Pilates, pressotherapy, laser, rehabilitation).

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition: on average 5 to 20 sessions for a complete program, at a rate of 1 to 3 sessions per week.

Do you see patients from outside Temsia / Agadir?

Yes — we welcome patients from across greater Agadir: Inezgane, Aït Melloul, Dcheira El Jihadia, Tikiouine, Bensergao and central Agadir. See our clinic location page.
