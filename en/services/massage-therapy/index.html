Therapeutic massage in Temsia, Agadir

Physiotherapy Clinic — Temsia, Agadir Our services Therapeutic massage

Massage therapy uses a variety of techniques to work on joints and soft tissues, improving overall body health. Massage increases the supply of oxygen and nutrients to your muscles, helping to eliminate waste from muscle tissues. It also accelerates the body's natural ability to heal itself.

General well-being and relaxation

Massage therapy is the assessment and treatment of soft tissue and joint dysfunction and pain. Massage can help restore flexibility and range of motion as well as increase overall well-being and relaxation.

All types of massage therapy

Treatment may include myofascial release, acupuncture / electro-acupuncture, therapeutic ultrasound, kinesio taping, massage gun, joint mobilization, and home exercises tailored to your situation.

Indications for therapeutic massage

Therapeutic massage is indicated for many clinical and wellness situations:

  • Muscular tension in the back, shoulders and neck (often related to desk work)
  • Sports recovery after training or competition
  • Chronic pain: lower back pain, neck pain, myofascial pain
  • Post-injury rehabilitation and post-surgical recovery
  • Stress and fatigue: improved sleep and quality of life
  • Edema and water retention (lymphatic drainage)
  • Migraines and tension headaches

Techniques practiced at our Agadir clinic

  • Swedish massage: deep muscle relaxation and circulation activation
  • Myofascial release: releasing connective-tissue tension
  • Deep tissue massage for chronic pain
  • Sports massage: preparation and recovery
  • Manual lymphatic drainage
  • Percussion massage gun therapy
  • Therapeutic taping to maintain session benefits between visits

How a session unfolds

Each session begins with a brief assessment (tension areas, history, goals) to personalize the massage. A session lasts between 30 and 60 minutes. You can book a one-off session (relaxation, recovery) or a multi-session protocol as a complement to physiotherapy.

Documented benefits

Beyond immediate well-being, regular therapeutic massage promotes better blood and lymphatic circulation, accelerates the elimination of muscle toxins, reduces cortisol (the stress hormone), and improves joint range of motion. It complements our other treatments perfectly: pressotherapy, laser therapy and clinical Pilates.
